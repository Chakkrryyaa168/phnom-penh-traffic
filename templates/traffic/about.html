Analytical Methodology & Mathematical Formulation

How hourly traffic volumes, peak hours, rush periods, and surge ratios are calculated

1. How Peak Hour is Calculated

In Phnom Penh Traffic Insights, an intersection's Peak Hour is defined as:

"The hour of the day (0 to 23) with the highest average traffic volume across all observed days."

Why Average Volume Instead of a Single Maximum Snapshot?

A single instantaneous maximum volume reading can be heavily skewed by one-off transient anomalies (e.g. an isolated accident, VIP convoy escort, or sensor glitch). By computing the expected average volume for each hour slot $h \in [0, 23]$ across all recorded calendar dates, the resulting 24-hour profile represents the consistent, structural daily traffic rhythm of each intersection.

Step-by-Step Algorithm (Executed via pandas)

  1. Hourly Grouping: All timestamp records for an intersection are decomposed by hour of day ($h = \text{timestamp.hour} \in \{0, 1, \dots, 23\}$).
  2. Mean Volume Computation: The arithmetic mean volume $\bar{V}_h$ is calculated for each hour $h$:
    V̄_h = (1 / N_h) * Σ volume(h, d)
  3. Peak Hour Identification: The peak hour $h^*$ is identified as the hour index maximizing average volume:
    h* = argmax_{h ∈ [0, 23]} ( V̄_h )
  4. Peak Range Formatting: Displayed as the 60-minute window, e.g. hour 7 becomes 07:00 - 08:00.

2. Rush Period Classification

Intersections are systematically classified into three operational periods based on their peak hour:

  • Morning rush 06-09 Hours 06:00 to 09:59 (6 ≤ h ≤ 9)
    #2a9d8f
  • Evening rush 16-19 Hours 16:00 to 19:59 (16 ≤ h ≤ 19)
    #e76f51
  • Other Midday, late evening, or night hours
    #e9c46a

3. Peak Ratio Metric

The Peak Ratio measures the relative intensity of the traffic spike compared to standard diurnal baseline traffic:

Peak Ratio = Peak Volume / (24-Hour Average Hourly Volume)
≥ 1.80x High Surge: Severe commuter bottlenecks with extreme tidal traffic.
1.40x - 1.79x Moderate Surge: Typical urban commute corridor.
< 1.40x Steady Flow: Continuous commercial or mixed activity without severe spikes.
