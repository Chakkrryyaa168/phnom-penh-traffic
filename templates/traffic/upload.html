CSV Format Requirements

The CSV file must contain the following 3 required columns (case-insensitive header):

  • intersection
    Name of the intersection (e.g. Monivong Blvd & Mao Tse Toung Blvd)
  • timestamp
    Date and hour (e.g. 2026-10-01 07:00:00 or ISO-8601)
  • volume
    Observed vehicle count in vehicles/hour (e.g. 1450)
Example Structure:
intersection,timestamp,volume
Monivong Blvd & Mao Tse Toung Blvd,2026-10-01 07:00:00,1250
Monivong Blvd & Mao Tse Toung Blvd,2026-10-01 08:00:00,1450
Russian Federation Blvd,2026-10-01 07:00:00,1650
